## API Updates

 * Paying a BOLT11 invoice (via `ChannelManager::pay_for_bolt11_invoice`) or sending a payment
   (via `ChannelManager::send_payment`) whose payee is our own node is now supported (#2462). When
   the router finds no route to ourselves, the payment is completed locally rather than over an
   HTLC: it surfaces `Event::PaymentClaimable` immediately with no preimage (as for a hold
   invoice) and registers a tracked outbound payment. Providing the preimage via
   `ChannelManager::claim_funds` then generates `Event::PaymentSent` and `Event::PaymentClaimed`.
   If the router does find a route back to ourselves, that route is paid as a normal HTLC.

## Backwards Compatibility

 * An unclaimed self-payment is tracked as a new `PendingOutboundPayment` variant, so it is
   persisted across restarts and appears in `ChannelManager::list_recent_payments`. Downgrading to
   an LDK version without self-payment support drops any not-yet-claimed self-payment, which is
   safe as no HTLCs are in-flight for it (#2462).
